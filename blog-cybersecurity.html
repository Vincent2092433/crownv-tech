What Is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, phones, networks, applications and digital information from unauthorized access, attacks and other threats.

Why Is Cybersecurity Important?

We use technology every day to communicate, learn, work, shop and store important information. This makes digital security an important part of modern life.

Good cybersecurity practices can help reduce the risk of losing personal information, having accounts compromised or having important files damaged.

Common Cybersecurity Threats

🎣 Phishing

Fake messages or websites designed to trick people into revealing sensitive information.

🦠 Malware

Malicious software designed to disrupt systems, steal information or perform unauthorized actions.

🔑 Weak Passwords

Easily guessed passwords can make online accounts easier for attackers to compromise.

How Can You Stay Safer?

You can improve your digital security by using strong, unique passwords, enabling multi-factor authentication where available, keeping software updated and being careful with unexpected links and attachments.

It is also important to regularly review your account security settings and learn how common online scams work.

Cybersecurity Is a Skill

Cybersecurity is not only about tools and technology. It also involves understanding risks, thinking critically and making responsible decisions when using digital systems.

Whether you are a beginner, student, developer or technology enthusiast, learning cybersecurity can help you become a more responsible and security-aware technology user.
